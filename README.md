# Dubai Beauty Brand Growth Intelligence

### Data-Driven Lead Research & Growth Opportunity Analysis

This project started with a simple question: **Which beauty and skincare businesses in Dubai have untapped growth potential, and how can we identify opportunities to help them grow?**

Instead of relying on assumptions, I combined web research, data scraping, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and machine learning to investigate 15 beauty, skincare, and cosmetics e-commerce businesses.

### What I Worked On

- Researched 15 Dubai-focused beauty and skincare businesses.
- Collected publicly available website and customer-review data.
- Analyzed customer feedback using NLP and sentiment analysis.
- Applied TF-IDF, K-Means clustering, and Isolation Forest to explore content patterns and unusual website characteristics.
- Investigated branding, product presentation, SEO, and potential conversion barriers.
- Ranked promising business leads based on observable improvement opportunities.
- Created a CEO-level research report with visualizations, findings, and recommendations.

### Tech Stack

**Python | Pandas | BeautifulSoup | Scikit-learn | NLP | Matplotlib | Jupyter Notebook**

### Project Outcome

The final output includes a structured lead database, research findings, ML-assisted analysis, an interactive dashboard, and a professional report highlighting potential growth opportunities.

**Note:** This research identifies observable digital weaknesses and possible growth barriers. It does not claim to verify declining sales or stagnant revenue without supporting financial data.

### Final Thoughts

For me, this project was about going beyond collecting business leads. I wanted to understand what the available data could actually tell us about a brand, its customers, and the opportunities it might be missing.

The goal was simple: **Turn research and data into meaningful business insights that support smarter decisions.**
